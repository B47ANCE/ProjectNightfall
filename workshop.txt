description=Project Nightfall
description=Project Nightfall is a UV-driven zombie overhaul that transforms daylight into a new survival threat. Sunlight forces zombies into hibernation—but darkness restores them, releases the Ferals, and gives Smart Ferals room to hunt.
description=Current Features
description=
description=- Sunlight damages zombies to a configurable health floor, then forces them into true fake-dead hibernation.
description=
description=- Hibernating zombies rise after nightfall and rapidly recover their health.
description=
description=- Artificial UV passes through regional clothing protection, causes persistent localized tissue loss and wounds, and remains lethal.
description=
description=- Overlapping UV lights create dangerous UV hotbeds.
description=
description=- Regular zombies are slowed by direct sunlight and artificial UV.
description=
description=- Trees provide configurable protection from natural UV.
description=
description=- Hibernators can be disturbed only at very close range and return to hibernation after disengaging.
description=
description=- Standard and high-output pure-blue UV bulbs work through lightWorks.
description=
description=- Artificial UV sources contribute independent damage, allowing overlapping lights to cook zombies faster.
description=
description=- Handheld, attached, weapon-mounted, and supported placed lamps can emit UV.
description=
description=- Marked infected corpses continue degrading under artificial UV through bounded emitter-local processing, with persistent remains stages while loot and cause of death stay intact.
description=
description=Ferals
description=Zombies must remain fully starved for seven continuous days before they become eligible to rise as Ferals. Feeding resets that starvation timer.
description=
description=- Ferals seek shelter during dangerous daylight.
description=
description=- Stored solar energy powers short, uncommon tactical sprints.
description=
description=- A full reserve provides up to five in-game minutes of sprinting.
description=
description=- Feral transformations include persistent bald, clean-shaven appearances.
description=
description=- Weapon-mounted, handheld, and attached UV lights can be used against them.
description=
description=Smart Ferals
description=Eligible Ferals can become intelligent predators capable of more deliberate behavior.
description=
description=- Stalking, flanking, hiding, ambushing, and tactical evasion.
description=
description=- Persistent grudges against players who attack them.
description=
description=- Local recruitment of regular Ferals into personal hordes.
description=
description=- Cooperation between Smart Ferals to form intelligent hordes.
description=
description=- Population-scaled follower limits and spaced formation movement.
description=
description=- Call-and-response screeches when Smart Ferals establish cooperation.
description=
description=- Shared investigation information without omniscient player tracking.
description=
description=- Smart Feral cognition can be set to zero to disable Smart Feral promotion.
description=
description=Alpha Ferals
description=
description=- Rare Alpha Ferals build persistent command hierarchies of Smart Feral-led hordes.
description=
description=- Alpha ecology includes calls, rivalry, desertion, Death Cry power vacuums, underground territories, and distributed Smart Feral dens.
description=
description=- Active artificial UV can compromise an underground Alpha den and physically force the Alpha into the closest safe shelter without deleting its territory or hierarchy.
description=
description=- Territorial losses accumulate migration pressure. Spring and summer favor migration, fall resists it, winter forbids normal migration, and rivalry always takes priority.
description=
description=Effigies
description=
description=- B47ANCEDHealthCare body parts retain source provenance and can build source-specific effigies.
description=
description=- Effigies preserve detached-part nutrition and drive transactional feeding, attraction, avoidance, destruction, and persistent Smart Feral hunts without omniscient tracking.
description=
description=No Ferals Mode
description=The included Project: Nightfall — No Ferals preset retains UV damage, optional sunlight knockdown, hibernation, and nighttime rising without creating Ferals or Smart Ferals.
description=Zombies rise again as regular zombies.
description=Sandbox Customization
description=Project Nightfall includes extensive controls for:
description=
description=- UV damage and health limits
description=
description=- Sunlight knockdown and hibernation
description=
description=- Nighttime recovery and rise timing
description=
description=- Feral transformations
description=
description=- Smart Feral eligibility
description=
description=- Sprint reserves and behavior
description=
description=- Canopy UV protection
description=
description=- Horde recruitment and capacity
description=
description=- Smart Feral communication
description=
description=- Artificial UV strength
description=
description=- Zombie awareness and shelter behavior
description=
description=The packaged Project: Nightfall preset configures the recommended experience and gives Nightfall control over zombie movement. Vanilla random sprinters, fake-dead spawning, and rally groups are disabled to prevent conflicting behavior.
description=

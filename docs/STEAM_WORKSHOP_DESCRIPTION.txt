Project Nightfall
Project Nightfall is a UV-driven zombie overhaul that transforms daylight into a new survival threat. Sunlight forces zombies into hibernation—but darkness restores them, releases the Ferals, and gives Smart Ferals room to hunt.
Current Features

- Sunlight damages zombies to a configurable health floor, then forces them into true fake-dead hibernation.

- Hibernating zombies rise after nightfall and rapidly recover their health.

- Artificial UV remains lethal and can permanently kill zombies.

- Overlapping UV lights create dangerous UV hotbeds.

- Regular zombies are slowed by direct sunlight and artificial UV.

- Trees provide configurable protection from natural UV.

- Hibernators can be disturbed only at very close range and return to hibernation after disengaging.

- Standard and high-output violet UV bulbs work through lightWorks.

- Artificial UV sources contribute independent damage, allowing overlapping lights to cook zombies faster.

- Handheld, attached, weapon-mounted, and supported placed lamps can emit UV.

Ferals
Zombies must remain fully starved for seven continuous days before they become eligible to rise as Ferals. Feeding resets that starvation timer.

- Ferals seek shelter during dangerous daylight.

- Stored solar energy powers short, uncommon tactical sprints.

- A full reserve provides up to five in-game minutes of sprinting.

- Feral transformations include persistent bald, clean-shaven appearances.

- Weapon-mounted, handheld, and attached UV lights can be used against them.

Smart Ferals
Eligible Ferals can become intelligent predators capable of more deliberate behavior.

- Stalking, flanking, hiding, ambushing, and tactical evasion.

- Persistent grudges against players who attack them.

- Local recruitment of regular Ferals into personal hordes.

- Cooperation between Smart Ferals to form intelligent hordes.

- Population-scaled follower limits and spaced formation movement.

- Call-and-response screeches when Smart Ferals establish cooperation.

- Shared investigation information without omniscient player tracking.

- Smart Feral cognition can be set to zero to disable Smart Feral promotion.

Alpha Ferals

- Rare Alpha Ferals build persistent command hierarchies of Smart Feral-led hordes.

- Alpha ecology includes calls, rivalry, desertion, Death Cry power vacuums, underground territories, and distributed Smart Feral dens.

- Active artificial UV can compromise an underground Alpha den and physically force the Alpha into the closest safe shelter without deleting its territory or hierarchy.

- Territorial losses accumulate migration pressure. Spring and summer favor migration, fall resists it, winter forbids normal migration, and rivalry always takes priority.

Effigies

- medWorks body parts retain source provenance and can build source-specific effigies.

- Effigies drive attraction, avoidance, feeding, destruction, and persistent Smart Feral hunts without omniscient player tracking.

No Ferals Mode
The included Project: Nightfall — No Ferals preset retains UV damage, optional sunlight knockdown, hibernation, and nighttime rising without creating Ferals or Smart Ferals.
Zombies rise again as regular zombies.
Sandbox Customization
Project Nightfall includes extensive controls for:

- UV damage and health limits

- Sunlight knockdown and hibernation

- Nighttime recovery and rise timing

- Feral transformations

- Smart Feral eligibility

- Sprint reserves and behavior

- Canopy UV protection

- Horde recruitment and capacity

- Smart Feral communication

- Artificial UV strength

- Zombie awareness and shelter behavior

The packaged Project: Nightfall preset configures the recommended experience and gives Nightfall control over zombie movement. Vanilla random sprinters, fake-dead spawning, and rally groups are disabled to prevent conflicting behavior.

Required Mods

- powerWorks

- lightWorks

- Project M.O.R.T.I.S.

Project M.O.R.T.I.S. supplies the reusable infected API and supporting systems. It does not add Nightfall’s zombies.
lightWorks supplies serviceable lights, colored bulbs, and UV emission semantics.
powerWorks supplies batteries, stored energy, and power-consumption calculations.
Compatibility and Testing
Project Nightfall uses vanilla zombie instances and is designed to preserve compatibility with ordinary zombie spawning and population settings.
Single-player development and testing are ongoing. Multiplayer compatibility is not currently claimed.

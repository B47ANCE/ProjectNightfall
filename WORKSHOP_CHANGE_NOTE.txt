[b]Version 0.27.4[/b]

[h2]Corpse processing fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Disabling Nightfall or regional UV now stops corpse discovery, death marking, and tissue processing. Re-enabling begins at current exposure time without charging the disabled interval.
[*]Corpse discovery preserves progress across bounded scan chunks and rotates across emitters instead of restarting or permanently truncating later work.
[*]Queued scan positions follow moved emitters and stop using removed sources.
[*]Rejected or throwing pipeline calls report failure without advancing the corpse clock or counting rejected work as successful. Other admitted corpses keep their processing turn.
[*]Pending journal recovery retains its actual committed time; failed remains-stage updates can retry without replaying dose.
[/list]

Offline regression suite passed. Dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED; stable release remains blocked. No new package or Workshop upload was performed for this source iteration.
